# Changing your plan

Go to **Billing → Change plan** and pick a new plan. The change takes effect right away and
your billing date stays the same.

## Upgrades are prorated

You've already paid for the current period on your old plan, so an upgrade only charges
the **difference in price for the time left** in the period.

> Example: you're on Pro ($29/month) and upgrade to Business ($99/month) with 6 days left
> in a 30-day period. The difference is $70, and 6/30 of it is left, so you're charged
> **$14.00** today. From your next billing date you pay $99.

The charge appears under **Billing invoices** as "Upgrade from Pro to Business (prorated)".

## Downgrades leave a credit

When you move to a cheaper plan, the unused part of the price difference is credited to
your account and shows as a negative billing invoice. Your new invoice limit applies
immediately.

## Restarting a canceled subscription

If your subscription was canceled, choosing a plan starts a new period today and charges
that plan's full price.
