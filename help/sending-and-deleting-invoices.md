# Sending and deleting invoices

## Sending

Open a draft and click **Send invoice**. Its status changes to **Sent** and it counts
toward your plan's monthly limit from then on.

A sent invoice that's past its due date shows as **Overdue** until it's marked paid.

## Deleting

You can delete any draft or sent invoice that hasn't been paid: open it and click
**Delete**. Paid invoices can't be deleted, because they're part of your records.

Deleted invoices disappear from your lists and **stop counting toward your monthly
limit**. Their numbers aren't reused.
