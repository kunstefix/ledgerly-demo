# Plans and invoice limits

Ledgerly has three plans:

| Plan     | Price                      | Invoices per month |
| -------- | -------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Starter  | Free                       | 5                  |
| Pro      | $29/month or $290/year     | 200                |
| Business | $99/month or $990/year     | Unlimited          |

Prices are the same number in euros for customers billed in EUR.

## What counts toward the limit

The limit counts invoices you **issue** in a calendar month (UTC). An invoice is issued
when you send it.

- **Drafts don't count.** Save as many drafts as you like.
- **Deleted invoices don't count.** If you delete an invoice, it no longer uses up your
  limit.
- The count resets at midnight UTC on the 1st of each month.

You can see how many invoices you've issued this month on your dashboard and at the top of
the Invoices page.

## When you reach the limit

Ledgerly won't let you create more invoices until the next month starts. To keep going,
[upgrade your plan](changing-plans). Upgrades take effect right away.
