# Cancelling your subscription

To cancel a paid plan, ask our support team in the chat window. We cancel at the end of
your current billing period, so you keep your plan until the period you paid for ends.

After cancelling:

- Your invoices and clients stay in Ledgerly. You can still view and download them.
- You move to Starter's limits (5 issued invoices per month).
- You won't be charged again.

Ledgerly also cancels a subscription automatically when a payment still fails after all
retries. See [Failed payments and retries](failed-payments-and-retries).

You can restart any time from **Billing → Restart subscription**.
