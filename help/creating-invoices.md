# Creating an invoice

1. Go to **Invoices → New invoice**.
2. Enter your client's name and email.
3. Pick the issue date and how many days your client has to pay. Ledgerly works out the
   due date.
4. Add up to three lines: description, quantity, unit price (net, before VAT) and VAT rate.
   The VAT rate starts at your country's standard rate.
5. Add notes if you like (payment instructions, a thank-you).
6. Click **Save draft**.

Drafts don't count toward your plan's monthly limit. When the draft looks right, open it
and click **Send invoice**. See [Sending and deleting invoices](sending-and-deleting-invoices).

Invoice numbers (`INV-0001`, `INV-0002`, …) are assigned in order and never reused.
