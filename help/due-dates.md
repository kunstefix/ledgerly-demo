# Issue dates and due dates

An invoice's issue date and due date are **calendar dates**. An invoice due on 20 October
is due on 20 October everywhere: Ledgerly shows the same date to you and your client,
whatever your timezone.

The due date is the issue date plus the number of days you chose ("Due in"), for example
14 or 30 days.

An invoice becomes **Overdue** the day after its due date, in your timezone.

Times, such as when an invoice was sent or paid, are shown in your account's timezone.
