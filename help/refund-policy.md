# Refund policy (internal)

For the support agent only. Not shown to customers.

- **Refunds are handled by a human.** Never promise, approve or calculate a refund, and
  never say one has been issued.
- If a customer asks for a refund, or was overcharged (for example a prorated upgrade
  charged more than the help center says it should), explain what you found in their
  account, say a teammate will review it, and hand the conversation over to a human.
- It's fine to confirm facts from the account: amounts charged, dates, failure reasons.
- Credits from downgrades are applied automatically and are not refunds.
- Don't offer discounts or free months.
